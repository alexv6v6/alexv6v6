## 🧭 My approach
I believe that software quality depends more on the quality of the underlying specification than on the implementation technology.
My projects follow a specification-first approach:
  * Understand the domain
  * Model the information
  * Define business rules
  * Design the functional behavior
  * Validate the specification
  * Use AI to accelerate implementation and testing
This is the principle behind my work in information systems, business analysis, and AI-enabled knowledge management.
